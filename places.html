Places to see

Our favourites, all within a short trip.

Top three

if you have a short trip, start here.

Christ the Redeemer (christ Redento)

The 38-meter-tall statue of Jesus Christ stands atop corcovado mountain offering panoramic views over the entire city.

Half a day

Sugarloaf Mountain (Pao de Acucar)

A towering granite peak at the mouth of Guanabara Bay.

Half a day

Ipanema Beach

Known worldwide through bossa nova culture, featuring vibrant beach sports, local snacks vendors and a lively neighborhood atmosphere.

Half a day

More to explore

  • Lapa: Visit Lapa for historic arches and live samba.
  • Tijuca National Park: The world's largest urban rainforest, great for hiking trailss, waterfall and spotting monkeys.
