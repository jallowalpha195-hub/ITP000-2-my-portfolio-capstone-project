Welcome to Brazil

Beaches,rivers and tourist attraction,all close together in Rio de Janeiro.

Why visit Rio de Janeiro?

Warm and expressive local people(Cariocas)

Locals, known as Ccariocas, place a strong priority on human connection and socializing in open-air spaces.

Lively Energy

People mix easily in the streets, sharing music, dancing and everyday life with an open, carefree spirit.

Famous Beaches

Relax or people watch along the the golden curves of Copacabana Beach and the fashionable shores of Ipanema Beach.

Quick facts

Capital: Brasilia

Money: reais (R$)

Language:Portuguese widely spoken
